CONCLUSION

The croods are a family whose life is based on survival, protection and staying together. Their greatest challenge is learning that the world is changing and that they cannot remain exactly as they want forever. Thier meeting with the bettermans introduces them to different way of life based more on inventions, confort and planning.

Although the two familie initially disagree, their experience teach them that different peaple can have valuable ideas. The Croods learn to become more open to change, while the betterman also learn the importance of cooperation and family unity.

Overall, the Croods presents family life in an entering way while teaching important lessons about love, communication, change, friendship, cooperation and respect for different ways of life.
