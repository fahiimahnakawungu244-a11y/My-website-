CROODS FAMILY BEFORE MEETING THE BETTERMAN THE BETTERMAN

At the beginning, the croods live a very simple and difficult life. They depend on nature food and shelter. They cave is thier main home and place of safety

Grug believe that the outside world is extremely dangerous. because of this, he creats many rules for his family. one of the his most important ideas is that they should not leave the cave unless it is necessary.

This creates a conflict with Eep. Eep is younger and curious, so she wants to discover what exists outside thier home, She feels that simple hiding from danger is not the same as living

This difference between Grug and Eep is important because it shows a common family problem: parents may want to protect thier children, while children may want independence and freedom.
